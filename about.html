Good to know

How early should I order?

Regular cakes: 24 hours. Designer/fondant: 3–5 days. Wedding tiers: 3–4 weeks. Same-day is possible for menu cakes — just WhatsApp us.

Are all cakes available eggless?

Yes — every single cake. We also make Jain-friendly versions without gelatin or root ingredients.

Do you do midnight delivery?

Yes, till 12:30am across central Ahmedabad. Candles and a knife are on us.

How do I pay?

50% advance via UPI to confirm, balance on delivery. Wedding orders: 50% at booking, rest a week before.

Can you copy a cake from Pinterest?

Absolutely. Send the picture in the design consultation and we'll recreate it — and usually make it better.
